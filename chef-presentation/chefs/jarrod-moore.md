## Biography

Executive Chef and Culinary Director of Melbourne's **Gathered Hospitality** group and **Nine
Yards Melbourne**, with **over a decade** building hospitality businesses and food events across
**Australia, Europe and the Middle East**. Driven by Australian produce and native ingredients,
and by making every menu and event fun, memorable and boundary-pushing.

**The journey**
- Stints in **Michelin-rated kitchens in Ireland**
- **Artist catering for Cirque du Soleil**
- Launched **Belleville Melbourne's** secret late-night, high-society supper club dinner series
- Private dining for members of the **Qatari royal family at the Royal Palace of Qatar**
- Co-founded the internationally renowned **Melbourne Chilli Championships**

**Events and collaborations**
- Menus for the **Melbourne Food and Wine Festival**, the **Australian Open** and **Australian
  Fashion Week**, many built on native Australian ingredients
- **Forgotten Fruits** — a sold-out foraging event gathering forgotten and seldom-used fruits and
  produce from **Victoria's High Country** for a high tea
- **Beasts of the Bush** — sold-out collaboration with Detroit chef **Montrice Shumpert** (The
  Botanical, Lagotto), native Australian produce meeting Southern American technique
- **Boodle Brunch** at Nine Yards Melbourne — with food media personality **Abby Marquez** and
  chef **Morris Danzen**, a Filipino boodle fight lunch blending Filipino and native Australian
  technique — 180 covers, and Abi Marquez's first service in a professional kitchen

**Television** — guest appearances on **My Market Kitchen** (Channel 10), **The Project** and the
**Today Show**, where he famously introduced the hosts to one of the world's hottest chillies live
on air.

**Now** — Executive Chef and Culinary Director for Gathered Hospitality across its **five venues**,
and **Culinary Ambassador** for Melbourne coffee brand **Rosso**, while continuing to develop food
events worldwide.

## Two threads worth pulling

**1. He has already cooked with a festival chef, in Melbourne.**
The Boodle Brunch collaboration was with **Morris Danzen** — who cooks the **Alma × CMD Supper
Club** dinner on Aug 27, the night before Jarrod's dinner at Lyma. Two chefs on consecutive
nights of this festival have a sold-out Melbourne collaboration behind them. **Confirmed from
both sides**: Morris was interviewed at that same Boodle Brunch — Nine Yards, Melbourne Food &
Wine Festival, 180 covers — see [morris-danzen.md](morris-danzen.md). Build the slide.

**2. Boodle, twice.**
The Melbourne event was a **Filipino boodle fight**. *Boodle* is festival vocabulary. A
Melbourne chef who has already staged a boodle lunch, flying in to cook a zero-waste coconut
dinner on the island the form comes from, is a stronger story than the biography line by line.

**A note on range.** Jarrod is the outlier in this library — Cirque du Soleil, a royal palace,
the Australian Open, a chilli championship, national television. Alma and Wild and Lyma are
island restaurants with island stories. The contrast is the point of a guest chef; don't
flatten it by presenting him in the same template.
