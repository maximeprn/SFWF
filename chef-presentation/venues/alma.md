## Chef Luis Martinez — biography

From the venue's "Our Story" page, in summary:

- Grew up in **Valencia**, where paella is everyday cooking and the waters are rich — the origin
  of Alma's menu.
- Learned to cook in his parents' kitchen. His father encouraged him to **sell paella around the
  neighbourhood at thirteen**.
- Opened the first version of Alma **in Spain, with his brother**.
- Came to Siargao on holiday, stayed, and opened **Alma on the island in 2020** — during the
  pandemic, when most restaurants were closing. It found its footing in the local community,
  tucked into a quiet residential neighbourhood overlooking the fishermen's boats.
- **Typhoon Odette forced it to close.**
- Opened **Terraza Martinez in Manila, September 2022**, moving toward more refined technique.
- Alma is now **open again in Siargao** — the full-circle line the venue itself uses.

That arc — Valencia, a boy selling paella, a pandemic opening, a typhoon, a return — is the
strongest narrative material found on any venue site so far.
