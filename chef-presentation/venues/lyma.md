## Chef Marc Silvestre Carbó — biography

The most complete chef biography of any venue site so far, and he has a page to himself.

- Born in **L'Escala, Catalonia**, a fishing town on the Costa Brava.
- **Moved to Siargao in 2017** and has stayed; the site describes him as one of the leading
  international chefs in the Philippines.
- Cooks **French technique + Spanish heritage + Asian influence** on **Filipino ingredients**.
- His method, in his own site's words: **fermentation-forward, zero-waste, farm-to-table.**
- Sources the daily catch from Siargao fishermen, produce from **Mindanao and the Cordilleras**.
- His framing of the work: food as connection — to the land, to culture, to the people at the
  table.

## The name

**Lyma comes from "lima" — five** — for five values the restaurant runs on:
**craftsmanship · sustainability · creativity · community · hospitality.**

They also make a point of vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free dishes getting the same attention as
everything else — worth a line if the presentation touches access.
