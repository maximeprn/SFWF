## If you edit these

- **Don't add adjectives to make a chef sound better.** Each of these already leads with the
  strongest true thing: Luis selling paella at thirteen, Morris cooking over wood at twelve,
  Marc's jars, Jarrod's royal palace, Wild's tuna. Superlatives on top of that read as padding.
- **Keep the numbers.** Thirteen, 2020, 44,000, five venues, five values. They carry the weight.
- **Two names still need confirming before print:** whether Morris goes by his full name
  Catanghal, and whether his Melbourne collaborator is *Abi* or *Abby* Marquez — the two
  sources disagree.
- Wild's is the thinnest, because the personal biographies are. It leans on the restaurant
  because that is what is documented. If the festival can get a line from either of them about
  their own cooking, that blurb improves most.
