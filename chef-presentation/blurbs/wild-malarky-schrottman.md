# Andrew Malarky & Jove Schrottman — Wild

*Aug 26 · Ani sang Siargao opening celebration*

Chef Andrew Malarky and Jove Schrottman run Wild through their Rude Boys Hospitality group,
pairing kitchen and hospitality between them.

Wild opened in 2019 on a backroad in General Luna with a small room, a short menu and a word for
what it was doing: *glocal*, global technique on local soul. It opened into a pandemic, and
Typhoon Odette flattened it a year later. They rebuilt it as the Guild Hall, built around long
communal tables where strangers are seated together.

What they do with it is the interesting part. Across two Tip to Tail dinners they have butchered
whole tuna in front of guests and cooked a menu from every cut, and they now grow their own
soft-shell and mud crabs in the Del Carmen mangroves, the third largest mangrove reserve in the
Philippines.

Wild opens the festival.
