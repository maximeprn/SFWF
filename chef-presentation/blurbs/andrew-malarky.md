# Andrew Malarky — Wild

*Aug 26 · Ani sang Siargao opening celebration*

Cooking a 500-cover weekend at Cluny Bistro in Toronto, Andrew Malarky was told he could take any
station in the kitchen. He chose garde manger — the cold line, usually a teenager's job. He hadn't
worked it in fifteen years.

That is roughly how he does everything. He spent thirteen years making Dos Mestizos into Boracay's
Spanish-Filipino landmark and spent its busiest nights out in the dining room instead of at the
pass. He moved to Siargao in 2019 because he noticed people there tended their gardens.

When the pandemic cut the imports off he cooked what grew nearby, and Wild has run that way since:
global technique, island produce. Octopus rolled and sliced into a carpaccio mosaic, an elevated
kinilaw. Shrimp on sugarcane with laing purée.

He tells his cooks not to learn the how without the why.
