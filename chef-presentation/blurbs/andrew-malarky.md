# Andrew Malarky — Wild

*Aug 26 · Ani sang Siargao opening celebration*

For thirteen years Andrew Malarky ran Dos Mestizos in Boracay, the island's Spanish-Filipino
landmark, where on busy nights he left the pass and worked the dining room instead. Later, cooking
a weekend of 500 covers at Cluny Bistro in Toronto, he was offered any station he wanted and took
garde manger, the cold line usually left to teenagers. He had not worked it in fifteen years.

He came to Siargao in 2019. What decided him wasn't the surf, it was noticing that everyone
tended their gardens and kept the island clean. When the pandemic cut off imports he learned to
cook from what grew nearby, and that constraint became Wild's method: global technique, local
produce. Octopus carpaccio sliced into a mosaic. Shrimp on a sugarcane skewer with laing purée.

At the first festival he butchered a sixty-pound tuna.
