# Jarrod Moore — guest chef at Lyma

*Aug 28 · with Marc Silvestre Carbó and Chef Jose*

Jarrod Moore is Executive Chef and Culinary Director of Gathered Hospitality, five venues across
Melbourne, and of Nine Yards. He has spent over a decade building restaurants and one-off food
events across Australia, Europe and the Middle East.

The list is unusual. Michelin-rated kitchens in Ireland, artist catering for Cirque du Soleil, a
late-night supper club at Belleville, private dinners for the Qatari royal family at the Royal
Palace. He co-founded the Melbourne Chilli Championships, and has built menus for the Melbourne
Food and Wine Festival, the Australian Open and Australian Fashion Week.

Native Australian ingredients run through most of it: a foraging dinner from Victoria's High
Country, a collaboration with Detroit chef Montrice Shumpert, and a Filipino boodle brunch cooked
with Morris Danzen, who cooks at Alma the night before Jarrod cooks at Lyma.
