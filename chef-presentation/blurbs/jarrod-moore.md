# Jarrod Moore — guest chef at Lyma

*Aug 28 · with Marc Silvestre Carbó and Chef Jose*

Michelin kitchens in Ireland. Artist catering for Cirque du Soleil. A secret late-night supper club
at Belleville. Private dinners for the Qatari royal family at the Royal Palace. Jarrod Moore
co-founded the Melbourne Chilli Championships and once fed one of the world's hottest chillies to a
breakfast television host, live on air.

He is Executive Chef and Culinary Director of Gathered Hospitality — five Melbourne venues — and of
Nine Yards, and has built menus for the Melbourne Food and Wine Festival, the Australian Open and
Australian Fashion Week.

Native Australian ingredients run through most of it: a dinner foraged out of Victoria's High
Country, a collaboration with Detroit chef Montrice Shumpert, and a Filipino boodle brunch cooked
with Morris Danzen — who cooks at Alma the night before Jarrod cooks at Lyma.
