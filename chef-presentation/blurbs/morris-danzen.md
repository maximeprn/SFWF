# Morris Danzen Catanghal — Alma × CMD Supper Club

*Aug 27 · with Luis Martinez*

Morris Danzen Catanghal started cooking at twelve, when his father went blind and he and his
younger brother took over the house. He cooked over wood, with whatever was there.

He meant to be a painter. He was a photographer, a videographer and a singer first, before a
kitchen claimed him: his wife's family ran a trattoria in Sicily, and he turned it Filipino.
Sweet potato, coconut milk and calamansi, cooked with Italian technique, in a town of 44,000
people who took some convincing. Then he won a televised cooking competition against Gino
D'Acampo, and the convincing was over.

He now cooks modern Italian at Buono in Melbourne and runs CMD Supper Club, his own Filipino
night, wherever a room will have him. Nearly fifteen years in, he still doesn't want a
restaurant. He wants a bigger radius.
