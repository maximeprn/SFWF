# Morris Danzen Catanghal — Alma × CMD Supper Club

*Aug 27 · with Luis Martinez*

Morris Danzen Catanghal learned to cook at twelve, when his father went blind and the house became
his and his younger brother's to run. He cooked over wood, with whatever there was. They ate once a
day.

He wanted to be a painter. He was a photographer, a videographer and a singer before any kitchen
claimed him — and then one did, in Sicily, where his wife's family ran a trattoria. He turned it
Filipino: sweet potato, coconut milk and calamansi, cooked with Italian technique, in a town of
44,000 people who took some persuading. He persuaded them by winning a televised cooking
competition against Gino D'Acampo.

He now cooks modern Italian at Buono in Melbourne, and runs CMD Supper Club — his own Filipino
night — wherever a room will have him. Fifteen years in, he still doesn't want a restaurant. He
wants a bigger radius.
