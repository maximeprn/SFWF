# Marc Silvestre Carbó — Lyma

*Aug 28 · coconut, zero-waste five courses, with Jarrod Moore and Chef Jose*

There are jars along the pass at Lyma — brines, preserved lemons, something deep red — and they
are the method rather than the decor. Marc Silvestre Carbó cooks fermentation-forward and close to
zero waste, which on an island means less arrives and far less leaves.

He comes from L'Escala, a fishing town on the Catalan coast, and has been in Siargao since 2017.
The technique is French, the inheritance Spanish, the seasoning Asian where it suits. The
ingredients are Filipino: the day's catch off Siargao boats, produce from Mindanao and the
Cordilleras.

He named the restaurant for *lima* — five — after the five things he built it on: craftsmanship,
sustainability, creativity, community, hospitality.

At the festival he cooks five courses, coconut, zero waste. The brief was written for his kitchen.
