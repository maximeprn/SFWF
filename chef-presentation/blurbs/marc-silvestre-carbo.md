# Marc Silvestre Carbó — Lyma

*Aug 28 · coconut, zero-waste five courses, with Jarrod Moore and Chef Jose*

Marc Silvestre Carbó comes from L'Escala, a fishing town on the Catalan coast. He arrived in
Siargao in 2017 and stayed.

Lyma is his. The name comes from *lima* — five — for the five things the restaurant runs on:
craftsmanship, sustainability, creativity, community, hospitality. The cooking is French in
technique, Spanish by inheritance and Asian where it suits, but the ingredients are Filipino:
the day's catch from Siargao boats, produce from Mindanao and the Cordilleras.

His kitchen is built around what it keeps. He cooks fermentation-forward and close to zero
waste, and the jars of brine and preserved fruit lining the pass are the method rather than the
decor.

The dinner he cooks at the festival is coconut, zero waste, five courses. His kitchen was
already built for it.
